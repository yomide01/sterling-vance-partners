How hair salons can show up when customers ask AI assistants for a local provider

Learn practical steps to make your hair salon visible when potential customers use AI assistants to find local services. Focus on foundational online presence.

Your Google Business Profile is Key

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is often the first place AI assistants look for local business information. It's essential to claim and verify your profile. This process confirms you are the legitimate owner and allows you to manage your listing directly. Without a verified profile, your salon's information might be incomplete or inaccurate, making it harder for AI assistants to recommend you.

Once verified, fill out every section completely and accurately. Include your precise business name, address, phone number, and website. Select the most relevant primary and secondary categories for your salon. List all your services in detail, from haircuts and styling to color treatments and extensions. Upload high-quality photos of your salon interior, exterior, and examples of your work.

Keep your GBP updated regularly. If your hours change, especially for holidays, update them promptly. Add new services or special offers. Responding to customer reviews on your GBP also signals an active and engaged business, which can be helpful for visibility. An up-to-date and comprehensive profile provides AI assistants with reliable information to share with potential customers.

Make Your Website a Local Resource

Your salon's website serves as a central hub for detailed information that AI assistants can draw upon. Ensure your website clearly displays your full business name, address, and phone number on every page, typically in the header or footer. Mention your city and specific neighborhood throughout your site where it makes sense, especially on your homepage and "About Us" page. This helps AI assistants understand your local relevance.

Create dedicated pages for each of your main services. Instead of just a general "Services" page, consider separate pages for "Haircuts," "Coloring," "Styling," and any specialized treatments you offer. On these pages, describe the service in detail, including any specific techniques, products used, or benefits. This rich, specific content provides AI assistants with more context about what your salon offers.

Ensure your website is easy to navigate and works well on mobile phones. Many potential customers will be searching on their phones, and AI assistants often prioritize mobile-friendly sites. A clear, fast-loading website with accurate information makes it easier for both human visitors and AI systems to understand your business and its offerings.

Consistent Information Across the Web

Beyond your Google Business Profile and website, your salon's information should be consistent across all online directories and social media platforms. This includes sites like Yelp, Yellow Pages, Facebook, Instagram, and any local business listings specific to your area. The key is to ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical everywhere.

Inconsistencies, even minor ones like abbreviations or different phone numbers, can confuse AI assistants. They might interpret conflicting information as a sign of an outdated or unreliable business, making them less likely to recommend your salon. Regularly audit your listings on various platforms to check for accuracy and make any necessary corrections.

Think of these online listings as individual data points that AI assistants gather to build a complete picture of your business. The more consistent and accurate these data points are, the more confident AI assistants will be in recommending your salon to local customers. This consistency builds trust and reinforces your salon's legitimate presence in the community.

Encourage and Respond to Customer Reviews

Customer reviews on platforms like Google, Yelp, and Facebook provide valuable insights that AI assistants can consider when recommending local businesses. These reviews offer social proof and detail customer experiences, which can help AI understand the quality and specific strengths of your salon. Encourage your satisfied customers to leave reviews by making it easy for them, perhaps with a gentle reminder at checkout or a link on your website.

It's equally important to respond to all reviews, both positive and negative. Acknowledging positive feedback shows appreciation and engagement. For negative reviews, a professional and empathetic response demonstrates your commitment to customer satisfaction and willingness to address concerns. This interaction signals an active and customer-focused business.

AI assistants can analyze the content of reviews to understand common themes, service quality, and customer sentiment. For example, if many reviews praise your "expert colorists" or "friendly staff," AI might pick up on these strengths. Your responses further enrich this data, showing that you value customer feedback and are attentive to your online reputation.

Detail Your Services and Specialties

When customers ask AI assistants for specific services, the more clearly you've detailed your offerings, the better your chances of being recommended. Go beyond general terms like "haircuts" or "coloring." List specific services such as "men's fades," "women's layered cuts," "balayage," "full highlights," "keratin treatments," "hair extensions," or "bridal styling."

For each service, provide a brief, clear description. Mention any unique aspects of your salon, such as using specific organic product lines, specializing in certain hair types (e.g., curly hair), or offering unique techniques. This level of detail helps AI assistants match customer requests with your precise capabilities.

Think about the specific questions a potential customer might ask an AI assistant. If someone asks for "a salon that does vivid hair colors," and your website or Google Business Profile explicitly mentions "vivid color specialists," you're more likely to appear. Specificity helps AI understand your niche and expertise, making your salon a more relevant recommendation.

Consider Emerging Signals: The llms.txt Convention

The digital landscape is always evolving, and new proposed conventions sometimes emerge. One such idea is `llms.txt`, a proposed convention for website owners. The intention behind `llms.txt` is to provide a way for webmasters to signal to large language models (LLMs) and AI systems how they would prefer their website content to be used or interpreted.

It is important to understand that `llms.txt` is currently a proposed convention. This means there is no guaranteed support for it across all AI assistants or large language models. Its adoption and how it might be interpreted are not yet universally established.

For now, focus on the foundational steps outlined in this guide. However, it is worth keeping an eye on developments like `llms.txt`. As AI technology continues to integrate into daily life, new methods for businesses to communicate with these systems may gain traction. Staying informed about such proposals can help you adapt your online strategy in the future, should they become widely supported.

Common questions

Do I need a fancy website?

Your website doesn't need to be overly elaborate or expensive. It needs to be clear, easy to navigate, and contain accurate, up-to-date information about your salon, services, and contact details. Ensure it works well on mobile devices. A simple, functional site that provides all necessary information is more valuable than a complex one that is hard to use or outdated.

How often should I update my online profiles?

Aim to review your Google Business Profile and other key online listings at least once a quarter. Update immediately if there are any changes to your hours, services, address, or phone number. Regularly adding new photos or responding to reviews also counts as an update and signals an active business. Consistency and freshness are helpful.

What if I get a negative review?

Respond professionally and promptly to all reviews, including negative ones. Acknowledge the customer's concern without being defensive. Offer to resolve the issue offline if appropriate. Your response shows potential customers and AI assistants that you are attentive to feedback and committed to customer satisfaction. It's an opportunity to demonstrate good customer service.
